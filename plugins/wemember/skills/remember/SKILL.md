# Remember in Wemember

1. Write down what to keep as one short, factual statement per item: the decision, finding or agreement itself, not the conversation around it. At most three items.
2. Leave out secrets, tokens, keys and personal data of customers. If the item cannot be stated without them, say so and save nothing.
3. Save each item with the Wemember tool `memory.remember`. Use personal scope unless the user explicitly asks to share it with a team or the organization; naming a project, a team or "we decided" is not such a request.
4. Confirm from the tool's receipt in one or two sentences: the title, who can see it, and that it awaits human review when the receipt says so.
